About

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Comparative Politics at the University of Bergen. My research looks at political parties and climate policy, with a focus on conservative and radical-right parties.

Before Bergen, I worked as a research assistant at the University of Barcelona (IBEI) and the Autonomous University of Madrid, on parliamentary agendas, climate and energy salience, and radical-right populism. In 2025 I was a visiting PhD candidate at UNC-Chapel Hill.
